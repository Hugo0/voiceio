// how it works

Speak. It types. Tonight, it learns.

01speak

Words land where you type.

Press your hotkey and talk. Text streams into the focused app — editor, browser, terminal — and commits the moment you stop.

02learn

It trains while you sleep.

Overnight, on your CPU, voiceio fine-tunes Whisper on your own recordings. The names and jargon you say most stop coming out wrong.

03prove

It only switches when it wins.

Each new model is scored on clips it never trained on. It goes live only if it beats yours. Rollback is one command.

// proof

One night of training. 60% fewer errors.

01same sentence, two models

Your names stop coming out wrong.

Stock models stumble on exactly the words you say most: people, products, your stack. A vocabulary helps on day one. Fine-tuning fixes them for good.

02word error rate
stock whisper-small12.1%
after one night4.9%
4.6×faster than realtime, on a laptop CPU — quicker than stock (3.6×).

44 held-out clips, 50 minutes of the author's dictation, scored against a larger Whisper's transcripts.

03measured, not promised

Run the numbers on your voice.

voiceio learn eval scores any model on your own clips: word errors, how often your terms come out right, and how long you wait after you stop.

Promotion needs a paired, clip-by-clip win, not a lucky average.

04private by construction

What it keeps, and where. All of it on your disk.

No account. No server. No telemetry. Logs record sizes and timings, never your words. Everything that holds your voice is off until you turn it on.

// install

Two commands to your first sentence.

Wayland and X11 — GNOME, KDE, Hyprland, sway, i3. voiceio probes your desktop and picks what works; voiceio doctor --fix repairs the rest.

debian / ubuntu 
# system packages, then voiceio
$ sudo apt install pipx build-essential python3-dev \
    portaudio19-dev ibus gir1.2-ibus-1.0 python3-gi
$ pipx install 'python-voiceio[desktop]'
$ voiceio setup
then 
# setup asks for your hotkey: press it, speak, press again
$ voiceio vocab add Kalshi     # a name, right away
$ voiceio learn schedule on    # learn every night
$ voiceio doctor               # what works, what doesn't

Fedora, Arch and NixOS: see the README. Python 3.11+. MIT licensed.
