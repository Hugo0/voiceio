Dictation that learns you.

Press a hotkey, speak, and the words stream into whatever app has focus. Everything runs on your machine — and overnight, voiceio trains a model on your own voice, so the names and jargon you say most stop coming out wrong.

It gets better at your words the longer you use it.

Speech models stumble on exactly what you say most: colleagues' names, your project, your stack. A vocabulary helps right away. Fine-tuning fixes it for good — on your laptop's CPU, while you sleep, with nothing uploaded.

  1. You dictate

    If you opt in, each recording is kept on your disk, next to its transcript.

  2. A teacher listens

    A bigger Whisper model re-transcribes your clips at idle priority.

  3. A model trains

    A small LoRA fine-tune on your voice, on CPU. No GPU required.

  4. It's tested

    Scored on clips it never saw, clip by clip, with a confidence interval.

  5. You decide

    A notification if it wins. voiceio learn promote switches; rollback is one command.

Turn it on once: voiceio learn schedule on. It runs nightly and never switches models by itself.

Measured, not promised

voiceio learn eval scores any model on your clips — word error rate, how often your terms come out right, and how long you wait after you stop.

Promotion has to win

A fine-tune replaces your model only when it beats it on everything; promote refuses otherwise and tells you why.

Your phone too

Run the same stack headless on a home server and dictate from your phone with your vocabulary, corrections and model.

Private by construction.

Speech is decoded on your machine. There is no telemetry. Logs record sizes and timings, never your words. What voiceio keeps is plain files you can read and delete — and the things that hold your voice are off until you choose.

From install to your first sentence.

Wayland and X11, GNOME, KDE, Hyprland, sway and i3. voiceio probes what works on your desktop and picks the backends itself; voiceio doctor --fix repairs what it can.

Debian / Ubuntu

# system packages, then voiceio
sudo apt install pipx build-essential python3-dev \
  portaudio19-dev ibus gir1.2-ibus-1.0 python3-gi
pipx install 'python-voiceio[desktop]'
voiceio setup

Then

# press Ctrl+Alt+V, speak, press it again
voiceio doctor            # what works, what doesn't
voiceio vocab add Kalshi  # a name, right away
voiceio learn schedule on # learn every night

Fedora, Arch and NixOS instructions are in the README. Python 3.11+.
