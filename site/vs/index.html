voiceio vs other dictation tools

voiceio is free, open-source voice dictation for Linux. It decodes speech on your own computer, streams a live preview into the focused app, and can fine-tune its model on your own voice. Here is how it compares with other dictation tools, with sources for every claim. Several are excellent on platforms voiceio does not support; we say so.

// faq

Questions

What is the best voice dictation for Linux?

It depends on what you need. For local dictation into any app on Wayland or X11, voiceio and Voxtype are the most complete open-source options; nerd-dictation is the smallest; Talon is for hands-free control on X11. Wispr Flow, Aqua Voice and Dragon do not run on Linux.

Which dictation tools work offline?

voiceio, Voxtype, nerd-dictation, Superwhisper with local models, and Dragon's locally installed Windows editions. Wispr Flow and Aqua Voice transcribe in the cloud.

How were these comparisons made?
