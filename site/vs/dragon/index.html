voiceio vs Dragon

Dragon, from Nuance (part of Microsoft), is long-established speech recognition for Windows, with locally installed editions (Professional, Legal, Law Enforcement v16), cloud editions (Professional Anywhere, Dragon Medical One) and a mobile app. It adapts to a single speaker's voice and offers full hands-free control of the computer. Dragon for Mac was discontinued in 2018 and there is no Linux version. voiceio is free, open-source dictation for Linux that also learns your voice, by fine-tuning a Whisper model on your recordings, but it is a dictation tool, not a hands-free control system.

Which should you use?

Dragon

Choose Dragon if…

  • You are on Windows and need hands-free control: open apps, navigate menus, mouse grid, macros and scripting.
  • You need an accessibility-certified tool (Section 508) that works with screen readers such as JAWS.
  • You work in legal, law enforcement or medicine and want a domain edition and vocabulary.
  • Your organization needs managed deployment, shared vocabularies and enterprise support.
voiceio

Choose voiceio if…

  • You are on Linux, where Dragon has no version.
  • You want something free and open source, with no license to buy.
  • You want voice adaptation that runs on your own hardware and that you can measure: voiceio learn eval scores every model on clips it never trained on.

Questions

Is there a Dragon NaturallySpeaking for Linux?

No. Dragon's current desktop editions require Windows 10 or 11, and no Linux client is listed (checked 2026-10-10). On Linux, voiceio, Voxtype and nerd-dictation provide dictation; Talon provides voice control on X11.

Does voiceio replace Dragon's voice commands?

No. voiceio handles dictation and a few editing commands ("new line", "scratch that", "correct that"). For hands-free control of the whole computer, look at Talon.

Does voiceio learn my voice like Dragon?

In a different way. Dragon adapts its profile as you dictate. voiceio keeps your recordings (only if you opt in), trains a LoRA fine-tune of Whisper on them on your CPU, and switches to it only when it beats the current model on held-out clips.

Is Dragon still available for Mac?

No. Nuance's support site says Dragon Professional Individual for Mac was discontinued effective 22 October 2018.
